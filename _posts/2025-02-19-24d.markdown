Due to the lack of data on contaminants in historic 2,4-D used on pasture and sports grounds in NZ 
from the late 1940s on offshore data from Ufa in Bashkortostan and Phu Cat in Vietnam was used
to identify the contaminant profile of high temperature and pre 1970 NZ 2,4-D as HpCDD and OCDD 
which were found at highest levels in sprayed NZ parks where grass was mulch mowed leaving behind
contaminants as opposed to pastures where grazing stock removed contaminants thru consumption.
