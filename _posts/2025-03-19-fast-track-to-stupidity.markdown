From 1969 to 1981 New Zealand Government subsidies on agrichemical defoliants used to clear brush and trees drove a substantial increase in agricultural land of 20,965 sq km.

<img src="/2025/03/19/this-iwd-product.png" width="100%">
<img src="/2025/03/19/the-subsidies-are-direct.png" width="100%">

<img src="crop-dusting-1.png" width="51.3%"><img src="crop-dusting-2.png" width="48.7%">

Or an additional 8% of the total NZ land mass brought into agricultural use [\[2\]](#2).

![nz-agricultural-land](nz-agricultural-land.png){:.centered}

In-between 1971 and 1973 dioxin levels in IWD 245-T decreased 20-fold and so ‘allowed’ for ongoing 245-T use.

<img src="average-levels-of-tcdd.png" width="50%">{:.centered}

In March 1972 the NZ Aviation industry cited 809 sq km sprayed from the air with around 20% of 245-T sold in NZ.

![as-assessment-of-the-area](as-assessment-of-the-area.png){:.centered}

In 1971 IWD production of 245-T was 265 tonnes - this increased to the 1979 peak in production of 808 tonnes [\[3\]](#3).

![chart-iwd-245-t-nz-defoliant-production](chart-iwd-245-t-nz-defoliant-production.png){:.centered}

In 1966-67 wool prices fell sharply by 40% and although they later recovered it was the start of a gradual decline.

<img src="horse.png" width="39.4%"><img src="spray.png" width="60.6%">

Between 1975 and 1985 wool exports increased by 45% and meat exports by 24% [\[4\]](#4).

Below hill country erosion after Cyclone Bola in 1988 seven years after the area of cleared agricultural land peaked at 58%.

![bola-hill-country-erosion](bola-hill-country-erosion.png){:.centered}

Assuming soil at 2.4 tonnes/m3 then North Island 2012 eroded exotic grasslands soils would cover 307 sq km to a depth of 10 cm [\[5\]](#5).

![chart-estimated-2012-nz-eroded-soils](chart-estimated-2012-nz-eroded-soils.png){:.centered}

Below ongoing hill country erosion after Cyclone Gabrielle in 2023.

![gabrielle-ongoing-hill-erosion](gabrielle-ongoing-hill-erosion.png){:.centered}

The NZ cost benefit analysis of soil losses versus increased production from land clearance has apparently not yet been reported?

![chart-estimated-2012-exotic-grassland-sediments-to-rivers](chart-estimated-2012-exotic-grassland-sediments-to-rivers.png){:.centered}

After USA NCI 1963-1968 ‘Bionetic’s’ animal experiments found that maternal exposures to both 245-T and 24-D herbicides caused birth defects in offspring by April 1970 US military use of Agent Orange in Vietnam ceased and restrictions on US domestic use were implemented [\[6\]](#6).

In NZ IWD first applied vacuums to reactors by 1970 reducing reaction temperatures and then two years after the USA a ‘Warning- Women of child-bearing age should avoid exposure to 2,4,5-T’ was recommended and 1971-73 IWD process modifications reduced dioxin levels in 245-T twenty-fold so ‘allowing’ for ongoing NZ 245-T use.
